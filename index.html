Quick workflow

A practical order for each Short.

  1. Write a strong first-line hook.
  2. Keep the quote tight: usually 3–4 lines.
  3. Record your voice and add captions.
  4. Use vertical footage and readable text.
  5. Track stayed-to-watch and average percentage viewed.

Hook checklist

Before recording, check that your first line creates curiosity, speaks directly to the viewer, and is understandable instantly.

  • Hook appears from 0.0 seconds.
  • No long introduction.
  • One central thought only.
  • Ending feels quotable or loops naturally.
